## Transport for the 2026 intake

During new-student reception, Xipu uses the west gate and Jiuli uses the south or west gate. Both campuses provide free internal shuttle buses. [Original welcome notice](https://ygb.swjtu.edu.cn/info/2331/6901.htm)

[Reception locations on all four campuses](/en/docs/freshman/introduction/)

## Eastern (International) Campus

Address: 88 Linqi Avenue, Eastern New Area, Chengdu. [Campus report](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_33586724)

The SWJTU–Leeds transport information identifies Exit B of Sancha station on Metro Line 18 for walking access to the campus. [Original transport information](https://leeds.swjtu.edu.cn/info/1044/22724.htm)
