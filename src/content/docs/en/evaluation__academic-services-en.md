## Undergraduate services by task

The “学生” section of the [Teaching Services Online Platform](https://jiaowu.swjtu.edu.cn/vatuu/WebSite?setAction=index) lists the services below. Open the relevant guide and check eligibility, documents and application dates before entering the application system.

| Need | Chinese service label |
| --- | --- |
| Transcript or proof of enrolment | 成绩单打印、学籍（在读）证明 |
| Question about a course result | 成绩复议申请 |
| Timetable clashes or examination arrangements | 冲突选课、缓考申请、补考报名 |
| Lost student ID or train-discount card | 学生证补办、火车票优惠卡补办 |
| Degree requirements, classes and exams | Under 信息查询: 培养方案、课程大纲、本学期课程安排、期末考试安排 |

Graduates and alumni have a separate section and should not assume current-student procedures apply. A listed service is not necessarily open year-round; also check [current Undergraduate School notices](https://bksy.swjtu.edu.cn/) and your school's requirements.

### Undergraduate proof of enrolment

The [proof-of-enrolment page](https://jiaowu.swjtu.edu.cn/vatuu/WebSite?setAction=detail&tspId=8E3746FC429F906F&tspSortId=61E92EF67418DC54) gives this paper-copy process: select the number of copies and complete online payment, then sign in at a self-service printer and select the successful application. **Each application can be printed only once.**

| Campus | Self-service printer location |
| --- | --- |
| Xipu | Beside the Academic Affairs Office on the second floor of the Comprehensive Building; Student Service Hall in Teaching Building 2 |
| Jiuli | Right side of the main entrance hall, first floor of Yifu Hall (逸夫馆) |

Academic Affairs Section contact: [028-66366252](tel:02866366252). For electronic certificates, open the “可信电子证明申请” link on the service page.

## Postgraduate certificates and training documents

The Graduate School provides a [trusted electronic certificate guide for proof of enrolment and Chinese/English transcripts](https://gsnews.swjtu.edu.cn/info/1957/24034.htm). The page was published on 2023-04-06, while its current attachment filename says **updated 2024-11-22**. Obtain the attachment from the page instead of relying on older screenshots.

The [Graduate School training downloads](https://gsnews.swjtu.edu.cn/xzzx/pygz.htm) include the postgraduate information-system guide and master's/doctoral training documents. Some list dates are older than the update dates shown in titles or attachments; check both.
