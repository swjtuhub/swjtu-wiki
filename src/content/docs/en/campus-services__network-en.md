## Choose the account you need

| Task | Entry point and requirements |
| --- | --- |
| Sign in to university applications | [SWJTU ID authentication](https://cas.swjtu.edu.cn/authserver/); undergraduate and postgraduate accounts are created automatically after student numbers are assigned |
| Activate campus internet access | Follow the [network account guide](https://inc.swjtu.edu.cn/wlfw/xywzh.htm) for preregistration, identity verification and payment |
| Check mobile and broadband bundles | See [operator internet services](https://inc.swjtu.edu.cn/wlfw/yysswyw.htm); billing is handled by the operator |
| Access university resources off campus | Use [SWJTU VPN](https://vpn.swjtu.edu.cn/) with your SWJTU ID; no separate VPN account application is required |

Account creation is described in the [official identity guide](https://inc.swjtu.edu.cn/xxhfw/tysfrz.htm). VPN access includes library literature and resources restricted to the campus network, subject to what the system makes available. See the [VPN guide](https://inc.swjtu.edu.cn/wlfw/VPNfw.htm).

## Wi-Fi and network activation

The published network name is `SWJTU-WiFi`. Authenticate with your campus account; wireless access itself requires no separate application. The [wireless guide](https://inc.swjtu.edu.cn/wlfw/wxsw.htm) includes Android, iOS and computer setup instructions. Its coverage description names Xipu, Jiuli and Emei; confirm Eastern (International) Campus access with the local service desk.

The [account guide](https://inc.swjtu.edu.cn/wlfw/xywzh.htm) asks new users to preregister in the [self-service system](http://service.swjtu.edu.cn/), then bring a student card or campus card together with an identity card to a service desk for activation. Do not add `@cer` or `@wt` to the username yourself. Account top-ups are also available in the self-service system.

> [!NOTE] Check the tariff year
> The operator page labels its tariff table “Autumn 2025”. Before applying, verify current prices, contract duration and cancellation terms rather than relying on that table or social-media advertising.
