## Eastern (International) Campus shuttles

The details below come from the official notice published on 3 September 2026.

### Timetable

| Direction | Service days | Scheduled departure | Scheduled arrival |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Eastern (International) → Jiuli/Xipu | Monday–Friday | 09:00, 13:00, 18:00 | 10:30, 14:30, 19:30 |
| Eastern (International) → Jiuli/Xipu | Saturday–Sunday | 17:30 | 19:00 |
| Jiuli/Xipu → Eastern (International) | Monday–Friday | 07:15, 11:00, 16:00 | 08:45, 12:30, 17:30 |
| Jiuli/Xipu → Eastern (International) | Saturday–Sunday | 09:00 | 10:30 |

**There is no service on national statutory holidays. Winter and summer vacation service will be announced separately.** Arrival times may vary with traffic.

### Stops

1. Jiuli Campus: South Gate roundabout, on the Electrical Engineering Building side.
2. Xipu Campus: General Building stop, near the flag platform.
3. Eastern (International) Campus: Eastern Campus shuttle stop, below “Heart of SWJTU” (交大之心).

### Tickets and boarding

- Advance booking: scan the official WeChat QR code to sign in to the Eastern (International) Campus service portal, then reserve a seat in the “校车出行” (Shuttle Travel) module. A one-way ticket costs **CNY 6 per person**.
- Refund limit: a reservation is valid only for that service on that date. No refund is available after the bus has departed.
- On-site tickets: if seats remain, pay by scanning with WeChat Pay or Alipay. Cash is not accepted.
- Boarding: arrive early, show the ticket QR code for that service, and sit in the reserved seat.

Get the booking QR code from an original post by one of the official university accounts listed below.

### Safety

- Do not board or leave the bus en route except in a special emergency, and follow the boarding rules.
- Flammable, explosive, toxic, corrosive, strong-smelling and other dangerous items are prohibited.
- In an emergency, contact safety officer Teacher Gou (苟老师): **13512251501**.
