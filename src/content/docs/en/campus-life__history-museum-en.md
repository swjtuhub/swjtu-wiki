## Group tours and preparation

The [regular-opening notice](https://dag.swjtu.edu.cn/info/1211/6901.htm) lists [028-66367520](tel:02866367520) for guided groups of at least 20 people. Its text is dated 2026-05-29, while the website list shows 2026-06-02. Its older opening days were superseded by the July notice. Call to confirm current tour availability, meeting point and arrangements rather than reusing the earlier fixed tour times.

That notice prohibits eating, drinking, flash photography, video recording, livestreaming and unauthorised guided tours. University events may temporarily affect access; check [Archives notices](https://dag.swjtu.edu.cn/listcolumn.jsp?a1088771c=10&a1088771p=1&a1088771t=8&urltype=tree.TreeTempUrl&wbtreeid=1211) before visiting.

Use the [campus maps](/en/docs/freshman/campus-map/) for navigation.
