## Orientation for the 2026 intake

The library notice dated 2026-09-04 schedules new-student orientation for **14 September–16 October 2026**.

| Item | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Booking | Schools, centres or laboratories book collectively |
| Time and place | The organising unit chooses and submits them; the library assigns a librarian to attend |
| Notice required | Submit at least one day in advance |
| Student preparation | Follow your school's meeting place, time and subsequent instructions |

The notice says students who have not received orientation can enter the library, but cannot use its space and resource services normally. Entry alone does not mean all services are ready to use.

Source: [Library notice reposted by Student Affairs](https://xg.swjtu.edu.cn/web/Home/Detail?xvw34vmu=010j_M3%3D5bc4cjb4-ejdg-eja0-bg5a-eb50jb1hh0fg.shtml), 2026-09-04. If unavailable, locate it by title in the [Student Affairs notice list](https://xg.swjtu.edu.cn/web/Home/PushNewsList?ManyPNodeNum=&PNodeNum=&PushPNodeNum=0109&page=1).

## Resources and services

- [Library website](https://www.lib.swjtu.edu.cn/): resources, services and opening notices.
- [Orientation booking system](http://bslc.lib.swjtu.edu.cn/rgjy/): for organising units; this is not individual seat booking.
- [SWJTU VPN](https://vpn.swjtu.edu.cn/): off-campus access with SWJTU ID; see the [official VPN guide](https://inc.swjtu.edu.cn/wlfw/VPNfw.htm).

The September–October orientation dates apply only to the 2026 intake. Consult current library notices for daily hours, seat rules and holidays.
