## Appointments and locations

The [Psychology Research and Counselling Centre](https://xinli.swjtu.edu.cn/) provides a “心理咨询预约” booking link. Its [current-semester duty roster](https://xinli.swjtu.edu.cn/info/1502/48536.htm), published on 2026-09-06, lists:

| Campus | Appointment telephone | Published location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Xipu | [028-66363086](tel:02866363086) | Teaching Building 2, Room X2340 |
| Jiuli | [028-87601810](tel:02887601810) | Counselling station, ground floor of Xiangzhangzhai Building 3 (香樟斋3号楼1楼) |
| Eastern (International) | [028-81361325](tel:02881361325) | Teaching Area C4, fifth floor, C4-0503 |

Use the booking link or telephone to check availability, then attend at the confirmed campus and location. The roster does not list Emei; ask the centre which arrangements apply.

## Current-semester arrangements

The roster uses three periods: 09:00–12:00, 14:00–17:00 and 18:00–21:00.

| Campus | Monday–Friday | Saturday | Sunday |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Xipu | All three periods Mon, Tue, Wed and Fri; morning and evening Thu | Morning and afternoon | No duty shown |
| Jiuli | All three periods Mon, Tue, Wed and Fri; morning and evening Thu | No duty shown | No duty shown |
| Eastern (International) | Morning and afternoon each weekday | No duty shown | No duty shown |

Individual counsellors and available slots remain subject to the booking system. Appointment numbers are not established as 24-hour support lines; see the separate “心理援助热线” section for hotline information. Holidays, temporary changes and full sessions may affect availability.

Counselling locations and hours follow the [2026–2027 first-semester counselling roster](https://xinli.swjtu.edu.cn/info/1502/48536.htm), published on 2026-09-06.
