## Collection points

| Campus | Service | Location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Xipu South | Temporary Cainiao station | West of Tianyouzhai 2 (天佑斋 2 栋), in the open space between the building and Logistics Support (后勤保障部, formerly 一服) |
| Xipu North | Temporary Cainiao station | Open space north of Canteen 5 (五食堂) |
| Eastern (International) | Parcel station | Student and Staff Service Centre (师生服务中心) |
| Emei | Postal service | Student Service Centre in front of the residences, formerly 七间门市 |
| Emei | JD and SF deliveries | Parcel racks at the student residences |

At Emei, Yunda handles deliveries for ZTO, YTO, J&T and other carriers. [Emei parcel-service notice](https://em.swjtu.edu.cn/info/1420/14096.htm)

## Opening date and scope

The posted Logistics Support Department notice dated 31 August 2026 states that the temporary north and south Xipu parcel points **opened on 3 September 2026** for parcel collection and delivery. These two locations are described as temporary. This notice applies to Xipu; the Eastern and Emei entries have separate sources below.
