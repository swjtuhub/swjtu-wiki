## Reception locations

| Campus | Where to go |
| --- | --- |
| Xipu | Yanghua Avenue by the West Gate, between the southern residences and sports hall |
| Jiuli | Passage between Juanchengzhai blocks 1–2 and 9–10 |
| Eastern (International) | Badminton hall, Sports Hall E |
| Emei | Covered sports ground |

During new-student reception, Xipu uses the west gate and Jiuli uses the south or west gate. Both campuses provide free internal shuttle buses. [Original welcome notice](https://ygb.swjtu.edu.cn/info/2331/6901.htm)

The 2026 undergraduate intake of the School of Smart City and Transportation and the SWJTU–Leeds Joint School reports to the Eastern (International) Campus. [Chengdu Fabu campus report](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_33586724)
