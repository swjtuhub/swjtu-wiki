## Email registration and login

| Task | Entry point or details |
| --- | --- |
| Student email | [Student mailbox](https://my.swjtu.edu.cn/), with addresses ending in `@my.swjtu.edu.cn` |
| Staff email | [Staff mailbox](https://home.swjtu.edu.cn/), with addresses ending in `@swjtu.edu.cn` |
| Register a personal mailbox | Open the [IT email guide](https://inc.swjtu.edu.cn/wlfw/jdyx.htm) and follow its “在线申请” self-registration link |
| Registration support | [028-66366559](tel:02866366559); the guide lists Xipu Library B156 |

Email accounts require real-name registration. Individuals normally self-register and may hold only one real-name account. If self-registration is unavailable, prepare the manual-application materials listed in the guide. Mailbox links are also available through the [university website](https://www.swjtu.edu.cn/jgsz/xzbm.htm).

## University cloud storage

The [cloud-storage guide](https://inc.swjtu.edu.cn/wlfw/jdyp.htm) gives the route “一网通办 → 交大云盘 → 个人云盘申请／部门云盘申请” for personal or departmental applications.

**Students should ask the IT office about eligibility and quotas.**
