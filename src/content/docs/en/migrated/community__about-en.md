SWJTU Wiki collects practical study and campus-life information maintained by the community. University departments' notices take precedence for official procedures.

## Contribute

Report issues or suggest corrections in the [original content repository](https://github.com/swjtuhub/swjtu-wiki). For dates, locations and procedures, include an official notice or original WeChat article and its applicable dates.

This edition uses Astro, Tailwind CSS and the Material Wiki theme, with articles maintained in Markdown.
