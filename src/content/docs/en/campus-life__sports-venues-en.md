## Temporary arrangements in September 2026

The Logistics Support Department announced these changes on **2026-09-02**. Check whether your venue is affected before planning exercise or an event.

| Campus and venue | Restriction dates in the notice | Access restrictions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jiuli outdoor basketball and tennis courts | 4–25 September | Temporarily closed to general access; online bookings disabled |
| Xipu table-tennis and badminton halls | 3–27 September | Closed for construction; general access and online bookings suspended |
| Xipu north stadium, volleyball and basketball courts; Jiuli stadium | 13–26 September | Closed during military training, except for participating staff, students and personnel |

Source: [Notice adjusting selected Jiuli and Xipu venue hours](https://hbb.swjtu.edu.cn/info/1018/82162.htm). All dates are in 2026 and apply only to the venues listed.

> [!IMPORTANT] Reopening requires confirmation
> Work at Xipu's table-tennis and badminton halls may change with weather or other conditions. The notice says reopening will be announced separately; it does not guarantee reopening on 28 September.

## Later changes

Check [Logistics Support Department notices](https://hbb.swjtu.edu.cn/tzgg/tzgg.htm) for reopening updates. Opening hours and booking requirements after temporary restrictions end depend on the latest notices.
