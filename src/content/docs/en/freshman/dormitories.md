## Electricity top-up

[SWJTU residence electricity-credit guide](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/rP4bsiSwyUsOIUtj6e3AIA), listed in the [official logistics index](https://hbb.swjtu.edu.cn/sjmt/wxgzh.htm).

1. Use the in-person electricity purchase point on the **first floor of Teaching Building 2**.
2. Search WeChat for “**交大购电微服务**”, bind the residence room and top up.

For a failed top-up, keep the payment record and contact the residence duty desk or logistics platform.
