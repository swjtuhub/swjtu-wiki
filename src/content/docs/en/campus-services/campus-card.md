The campus card is used for on-campus payments and identification. It is a separate service from a mobile phone plan sold by a telecom operator; see the [network guide](/en/docs/campus-services/network/). [Official campus card guide](https://inc.swjtu.edu.cn/wlfw/xyykt.htm)

## Service desks

| Campus | IT campus card desk |
| --- | --- |
| Xipu | Library, first floor, Zone B |
| Jiuli | IT office, first-floor service desk |
| Emei | Middle School Building (中学楼), first-floor IT service hall |

Bring your own valid identification when requesting service. Published card-service numbers: [028-87600976](tel:02887600976), [028-66366569](tel:02866366569), [0833-5198395](tel:08335198395). The guide does not list an Eastern (International) Campus desk; ask your school or campus service desk for that location. [Locations, ID requirements and contacts](https://inc.swjtu.edu.cn/wlfw/xyykt.htm)

## Top-ups and enquiries

Use the QR code in the [university WeCom guide](https://inc.swjtu.edu.cn/xxhfw/qywx.htm) to access the mobile portal and find campus card services. Initial binding requires university authentication and phone verification; the service is for SWJTU staff and students.

The [card guide](https://inc.swjtu.edu.cn/wlfw/xyykt.htm) also lists Alipay and Bestpay (翼支付), with an instruction to collect the credited amount by tapping the card at a canteen POS terminal after those top-ups. First check that the application still offers SWJTU services. Keep transaction details and contact a desk if the balance does not arrive or the card has a problem.

## WeCom services

The IT office lists campus cards, network top-ups, email, repairs and campus information among its applications. For binding or authentication support, the [WeCom guide](https://inc.swjtu.edu.cn/xxhfw/qywx.htm) lists [028-66366567](tel:02866366567) and [028-66366978](tel:02866366978).
